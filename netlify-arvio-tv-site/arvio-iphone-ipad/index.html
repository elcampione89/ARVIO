Three minutes

Set up ARVIO on your iPhone or iPad.

  1. Open web.arvio.tv in Safari.

    Type web.arvio.tv into the address bar. Safari gives the most reliable Home Screen experience on iPhone and iPad.

  2. Sign in with your ARVIO account.

    Use the same email you use in the ARVIO app on Android or TV, then choose your profile. Your profiles, watchlist, Continue Watching and progress come across through ARVIO Cloud. No ARVIO account yet? Create one on the sign-in screen.

  3. Add it to your Home Screen.

    Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. On recent iOS versions, Share sits behind the ••• button next to the address bar; on iPad it is at the top right. If Safari shows Open as Web App, leave it on. ARVIO now opens from its own icon, full screen and without the Safari toolbar.

Look around before you pay.

Once you are signed in, ARVIO Web is yours to explore. You can choose a profile, browse your watchlist, catalogs and title pages, and set up addons, home servers and your live TV playlist. Everything you set up stays in your ARVIO account.

ARVIO Web asks you to choose a plan only when you press Play. If you decide not to, nothing is charged.

Check playback on the device you use.

Safari plays common formats such as MP4 with H.264 or HEVC video and AAC audio. Some containers and audio formats, such as MKV, DTS or TrueHD, may not play in the browser. For those, ARVIO Web can hand the source off to VLC on iPhone and iPad.

Test one title you know your account can play before setting up everything else. If browsing works but playback does not, the browser playback checklist separates access, connection and format problems.

Software, not content

ARVIO is a media hub for sources you configure. It does not host, provide, sell or include movies, channels, playlists or third-party subscriptions. Connect only services and media you are authorized to use.

Common questions

iPhone and iPad questions.

Is there an ARVIO app in the App Store?

No. On iPhone and iPad, ARVIO runs as ARVIO Web in Safari. Add it to your Home Screen to open it like an app. The Android APK cannot be installed on iOS or iPadOS.

Do I need a new account?

No. Sign in with the ARVIO account you use on Android or TV. Your profiles, watchlist and progress sync through ARVIO Cloud.

What can I do before I pay?

Sign in, choose a profile, browse your watchlist and catalogs, and set up addons, home servers and live TV. ARVIO Web asks you to choose a plan when you press Play.

What if it does not work on my iPhone or iPad?

Message us on Discord within 7 days of paying and we refund you in full.

Does ARVIO include movies or channels?

No. ARVIO is a media hub for sources you configure. It does not include movies, channels or third-party subscriptions. Connect only services and media you are authorized to use.
